Our story

Kiku Studio builds handmade Mvule and Mahogany furniture to order at our Nairobi workshop — sofas, beds, dining sets, TV stands and coffee tables — and delivers across Kenya. We confirm price and delivery on WhatsApp before you order. Visit us on Getathuru Road to see pieces in person.

How ordering works

  1. 1

    Tell us what you need

    Send us the design, dimensions and a photo of what you have in mind.

  2. 2

    We confirm the price

    We reply on WhatsApp with the price and delivery quote for your town — before you order.

  3. 3

    We build it

    Your piece is made to order at our Nairobi workshop. We confirm the build time when you order.

  4. 4

    Delivered to your door

    Delivery across Kenya — cost and time confirmed for your destination.
